n8n PRACTICAL WORKFLOW 3 AI LEAD QUALIFICATION WITH HUMAN APPROVAL For
non-technical learners

PURPOSE Automatically understand a new lead, extract important
information, categorize the lead, store it, and ask a human for approval
before sending an important response.

REAL-WORLD EXAMPLE A company receives: “I am from ABC Pvt Ltd. We have
50 employees and need AI training. Our budget is around Rs 2 lakh. We
want training next month.”

AI should understand: - Company - Employee count - Requirement -
Budget - Timing - Lead priority

Then: AI -> Qualification -> Human approval -> Email

SYLLABUS COVERAGE Module 1: Agents Module 2: Structured workflows Module
4: Integrations Module 7: Governance and human oversight

BASIC SETUP Create Google Sheet: Name: Lead Management Sheet: Sheet1
Columns: Name | Email | Company | Requirement | Employees | Budget |
Timeline | Lead Type | Priority | Status | Notes

Connect: OpenAI Google Sheets Gmail Optional Slack Optional human
approval mechanism supported by your n8n version

STEP 1 - CREATE WORKFLOW Name: AI Lead Qualification

STEP 2 - ADD FORM TRIGGER Use: Form Trigger

Fields: Name Email Company Requirement Employees Budget Timeline

Keep the form simple.

STEP 3 - ADD AI AGENT OR INFORMATION EXTRACTOR Purpose: Turn form text
and fields into structured business information.

System Message: You are a lead qualification assistant.

Extract only the information provided by the user.

Return: name email company requirement employees budget timeline
lead_type priority

Do not invent missing values.

Lead type must be: Corporate Individual Unknown

Priority must be: High Medium Low

STEP 4 - STRUCTURED OUTPUT Use a structured output/parser option if
available.

Expected: { “name”: “Rahul”, “email”: “rahul@example.com”, “company”:
“ABC Pvt Ltd”, “requirement”: “AI training”, “employees”: 50, “budget”:
200000, “timeline”: “Next month”, “lead_type”: “Corporate”, “priority”:
“High” }

STEP 5 - ADD VALIDATION Use IF or Switch.

Check: - Email exists - Requirement exists - Lead type is valid - Budget
is a number if provided

If invalid: Send to a manual review/error branch.

STEP 6 - STORE LEAD Google Sheets: Operation: Append Row

Map every extracted field.

Status: Needs Approval

IMPORTANT: Append Row adds a new lead. Get Row(s) is for reading
existing records.

STEP 7 - HUMAN APPROVAL Add the n8n approval/human review mechanism
available in your version.

Purpose: The AI can prepare the recommendation, but a human decides
whether to contact the lead.

Approval message: New high-priority lead:

Company: [company] Requirement: [requirement] Budget: [budget] Timeline:
[timeline]

Approve sending the follow-up email?

STEP 8 - APPROVED BRANCH If approved: Gmail -> Send Email

Example: Hello [Name],

Thank you for your enquiry about [Requirement]. We have received your
request and our team will contact you shortly.

Regards, Training Team

STEP 9 - REJECTED BRANCH If not approved: Update Google Sheet: Status =
Rejected / Needs Review

Do not send the customer email.

STEP 10 - UPDATE STATUS Approved: Status = Contacted

Rejected: Status = Not Contacted

STEP 11 - OPTIONAL SALES NOTIFICATION Send Slack/Gmail notification: New
approved high-priority lead received.

STEP 12 - TEST CASES

Test 1: Corporate: 50 employees, budget Rs 200000. Expected: Corporate /
High

Test 2: Individual student: One learner, small budget. Expected:
Individual / Medium or Low depending on rules.

Test 3: Missing email. Expected: Validation failure -> Human review.

TEACHING POINT AI should not directly make every business decision.

Safer enterprise pattern: Input -> AI extraction -> Validation -> Human
approval -> Action -> Logging

COMMON PROBLEMS AI invents a budget: Tell it to return null/unknown when
data is missing.

Approval never arrives: Check the approval node and its
credentials/notification method.

Wrong Google Sheet row: Verify field mapping and use Append Row for new
leads.

EMAIL IS SENT TOO EARLY: Make sure the Gmail node is AFTER the approval
branch.

FINAL FLOW Form -> AI Extraction -> Structured Output -> Validation ->
Google Sheets -> Human Approval -> Approved? Yes -> Gmail -> Update
Sheet No -> Update Sheet
