n8n PRACTICAL WORKFLOW 6 MULTI-AGENT BUSINESS RESEARCH TEAM Supervisor +
Specialist Agents + Reviewer For non-technical learners

PURPOSE Teach why multiple specialized AI agents can be better organized
than one agent doing every task.

REAL-WORLD EXAMPLE User: “I want to open a coffee shop in Surat with a
budget of Rs 10 lakh. Research the market, competitors, customers, and
prepare a concise report.”

Instead of one AI doing everything: Supervisor delegates.

FINAL ARCHITECTURE

User | v Supervisor Agent | +–> Market Research Agent –> Brave Search |
+–> Competitor Research Agent –> Brave Search | +–> Customer Research
Agent –> Brave Search | v Report Agent | v Reviewer Agent | v Human
Approval | v Gmail / Final Output

SYLLABUS COVERAGE Module 2: Workflow design Module 5: Multi-step
pipelines Module 6: Multi-agent collaboration Module 7: Human oversight
and reliability

IMPORTANT CONCEPT Tools = capabilities. Agents = responsibilities.
Multi-agent system = specialized responsibilities coordinated together.

WORKFLOW 1 - MAIN SUPERVISOR

Name: Market Research Agent

STEP 1 - TRIGGER Node: When Chat Message Received

STEP 2 - SUPERVISOR AI AGENT Node: AI Agent

Add: OpenAI Chat Model Simple Memory if conversation context is useful.

Supervisor System Message: You are the Supervisor Agent for a business
research team.

Delegate only the work that is required.

Specialists: 1. Market Research Agent: market demand, trends, industry
information, statistics.

2.  Competitor Research Agent: competitors, locations, products,
    pricing, online presence.

3.  Customer Research Agent: target customers, preferences, pain points,
    price sensitivity.

Rules: - Use the appropriate specialist. - If the request needs multiple
research areas, call the required specialists. - Do not repeat the same
specialist. - Do not perform detailed research yourself when a
specialist is available. - Keep requests concise. - Ask specialists for
maximum 5 findings and maximum 3 important sources. - Combine results
only after specialist research is complete.

STEP 3 - ADD MARKET WORKFLOW TOOL Tool: Call n8n Workflow Tool

Workflow: Market Research Agent part-2

Input: research_request Defined automatically by model.

Tool description: Use this tool to research market demand, trends,
industry information, statistics, and opportunities for the user’s
business idea.

STEP 4 - MARKET SPECIALIST WORKFLOW New workflow: Market Research Agent
part-2

Trigger: When Executed by Another Workflow

Input: research_request Type: String

AI Agent: Prompt/User Message: {{ $json.research_request }}

System: You are the Market Research Specialist. Research only market
trends, demand, industry information, statistics, and opportunities. Use
Brave Search. Maximum 3 targeted searches. Return maximum 5 findings and
maximum 3 sources. Do not invent facts.

Add: OpenAI Chat Model Brave Search

Publish the workflow.

STEP 5 - ADD COMPETITOR TOOL Main workflow: Call n8n Workflow Tool

Workflow: Competitor Research Agent part-2

Input: research_request Defined automatically by model.

Description: Research competitors, locations, products, services,
pricing, online presence, reviews, and differentiators.

STEP 6 - COMPETITOR SPECIALIST New workflow: Competitor Research Agent
part-2

Trigger: When Executed by Another Workflow

Input: research_request String

AI Agent: Prompt: {{ $json.research_request }}

System: You are the Competitor Research Specialist. Research only
competitors and competitor information. Use Brave Search. Maximum 3
targeted searches. Return maximum 5 findings and maximum 3 sources. Do
not invent facts.

Add: OpenAI Chat Model Brave Search

Publish.

STEP 7 - CUSTOMER SPECIALIST Create: Customer Research Agent part-2

Trigger: When Executed by Another Workflow

Input: research_request String

AI Agent Prompt: {{ $json.research_request }}

System: You are the Customer Research Specialist. Research target
customer segments, preferences, pain points, price sensitivity, and
customer demand indicators. Use Brave Search. Maximum 3 targeted
searches. Return maximum 5 findings and maximum 3 sources. Do not
research competitors unless necessary to understand customers.

Add: OpenAI Chat Model Brave Search

Publish.

STEP 8 - CONNECT CUSTOMER TOOL In main Supervisor: Add Call n8n Workflow
Tool.

Workflow: Customer Research Agent part-2

Input: research_request Defined automatically by model.

Description: Research target customers, preferences, pain points, price
sensitivity, and customer demand indicators.

STEP 9 - TEST THE THREE AGENTS Use: “I want to open a small coffee shop
in Surat with a budget of Rs 10 lakh. Research the market, main
competitors, and target customers.”

Expected: Supervisor calls all three specialists.

STEP 10 - REPORT AGENT After collecting specialist outputs, create a
Report Agent.

Purpose: Combine research into a concise report.

System: You are a Business Report Writer. Use only the research supplied
by the specialist agents. Do not add unsupported facts. Create:
Executive Summary Market Findings Competitor Findings Customer Findings
Key Numbers Sources

Keep it concise.

STEP 11 - REVIEWER AGENT Create: Reviewer Agent

Purpose: Check the report before delivery.

System: You are a report quality reviewer.

Check: 1. Are claims supported by supplied research? 2. Are important
sections missing? 3. Are there contradictions? 4. Are numbers clearly
identified? 5. Are sources present?

Return: PASS or REVISE and maximum 5 corrections.

STEP 12 - HUMAN APPROVAL Send the final report to a human reviewer.

Ask: “Approve this report for delivery?”

Approved: Gmail -> Send report.

Rejected: Return to correction/review branch.

STEP 13 - TOKEN-SAVING RULES Supervisor: - delegate only necessary
specialists - call each specialist once - keep instructions short

Specialists: - maximum 3 targeted searches - maximum 5 findings -
maximum 3 sources

Report Agent: - do not perform new research - use only specialist
outputs

Reviewer: - do not rewrite the entire report - return only PASS or
corrections

STEP 14 - TEACHING DEMONSTRATION Run these three requests:

A: “Research the market for a coffee shop in Surat.” Expected: Market
Agent only.

B: “Find competitors for a coffee shop in Surat.” Expected: Competitor
Agent only.

C: “Research market, competitors, and customers for a coffee shop in
Surat.” Expected: All three specialists.

This clearly demonstrates why the Supervisor exists.

COMMON PROBLEMS Specialist is not called: Check that the workflow is
Published and the Call n8n Workflow Tool points to the correct workflow.

Specialist receives null: Check the Workflow Input Schema and map
research_request.

Supervisor answers from its own knowledge: Strengthen system
instructions: use specialist tools for detailed research.

Too many tokens: Reduce searches and output limits.

Final report invents facts: Tell Report Agent to use only specialist
outputs.

FINAL TEACHING FLOW User -> Supervisor -> Specialized Agents -> Research
-> Report -> Review -> Human Approval -> Delivery

KEY LESSON One agent can have many tools. But when responsibilities
become different, specialized agents can make the system easier to
organize, control, and extend.
